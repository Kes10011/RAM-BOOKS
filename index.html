About the Author

I am a therapist by profession. I attended BMCC and New York City College of Technology, where I placed second for a Charles Matusik Fiction Award for my piece “The Snow Globe,” an excerpt of which has been published in City Tech Writer, Volume 9 (2014). I have also taken Fiction Writing workshops and seminars as part of Columbia University’s continuing education program, and have experimented with writing poetry, songs, short stories, and novels across different genres and styles.
